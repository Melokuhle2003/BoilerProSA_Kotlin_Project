# BoilerPro SA v3 Admin Functions

## ADMIN
- Full dashboard
- Create/edit/publish/unpublish/delete lessons
- Upload lesson images
- Create/edit/delete practice questions
- Manage learner/instructor/admin accounts
- View audit log

## INSTRUCTOR
- View dashboard
- Create/edit lessons
- Upload lesson images
- Create/edit practice questions
- Cannot manage accounts
- Cannot view the full audit log
- Cannot delete lessons/questions

## LEARNER
- Uses the published lesson/question APIs only.

## Content policy for BoilerPro SA

Current scope is boilermaking/fabrication trade-test preparation. Do not add welding, welding theory, welding simulator content or engineering drawing content unless the product scope is explicitly changed.

User-supplied fabrication principles should be labelled as training principles/custom content where appropriate and checked against the actual job specification, approved procedure and applicable South African training requirements before being presented as an official rule.
